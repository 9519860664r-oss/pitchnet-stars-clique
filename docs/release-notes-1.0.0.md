## PitchNet Stars Clique Edition 1.0.0

The first release of the Stars Clique edition of PitchNet, a neural vocal pitch editor for VST3 hosts.

PitchNet was created by [Session Loops and contributors](https://github.com/SessionLoops/PitchNet) and is based on [HachiTune](https://github.com/KCKT0112/HachiTune). This edition is a modified version by Goth Angel (Mikhail Mikhalchenko), Stars Clique.

### Download

### Highlights

- **Multitrack in FL Studio and REAPER**: one track list for every PitchNet track; edit any number of tracks at once, show the rest as grey ghosts. In REAPER it works through ARA, clip by clip on the timeline.
- **Recording from the DAW (Transfer)**: several tracks in one pass, punch in/out, re-record any section, replace ranges.
- **Lead and doubles**: group a lead with its doubles; **Unison** puts them on the lead's notes while each keeps its own vibrato and transitions (no clicks between notes, octave doubles stay in their octave), and **Timing** lines them up syllable by syllable, live, even with their windows closed.
- **Sibilants**: "s", "sh", "t", "k" and breaths are drawn hatched and keep their own sound when a note is tuned and their length when it is stretched; Sibilant Balance per note.
- **Backing vocals to a reference**: Align to Reference (timing, pitch, level), tune in unison or an interval, Quantize Time, Leveling.
- **Note Inspector**: type pitch, vibrato, drift, formant and level; Note Off.
- **Keys and tuning**: one shared key, key changes along the song, A4 400–480 Hz, custom scales with cents.
- **Navigation**: wheel and modifier zoom, hand and zoom tools, zoom from the ruler, fit all.
- **Fast rendering on the GPU**: every track renders on the high-performance GPU when there is one.
- **New Stars Clique skin** (deep violet glass) plus the Classic look.
